$./follow-a-request.sh

Follow a request

This is how my homelab decides what gets in from the Internet. My home network opens no ports: published services go out through a VPS and a tunnel that the home server starts itself. Pick who’s calling and see which layer stops it.

  1. Geo-blocking

    The VPS firewall only lets HTTPS in from Spain.

  2. CrowdSec

    Bans IPs based on behaviour and shared reputation.

  3. Reverse proxy

    TLS with Let’s Encrypt, and only the published hostnames.

  4. WireGuard tunnel

    Allow-list: it reaches the published services and nothing else on the network.

  5. Passkey login

    Passwordless OIDC SSO, or a second factor.

  6. Service

    Photos, files and documents that live at home.
